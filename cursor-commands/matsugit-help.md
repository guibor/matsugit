# What can I do with matsugit?

Use the matsugit skill. Answer for someone who is comfortable in PowerPoint and
new to Markdown. Keep it short, concrete and free of syntax; offer to do the
step for them. Adapt to what they already have open.

Cover, in this order:
- One file is both the slides and the reading document. The theme does the
  formatting; they write the content.
- What to ask you for: "turn these notes into a deck" (`/matsugit-new`), "add a
  slide comparing two options", "make this a chart", "tighten this slide",
  "what is wrong with my deck" (`/matsugit-check`).
- What they can do themselves in the workspace (`/matsugit-slides` opens it):
  type on the left and watch the slide on the right; `Add` picks a slide or
  layout by picture; `Check` lists problems by slide with Show, a one-click fix
  and Ask AI; Story rearranges slides (`/matsugit-story`); the screen icon
  presents; the download icon exports PowerPoint or PDF.
- The layouts by name: blank slide, cover, section heading, big statement,
  answer first, table and what it means, evidence beside its meaning, chart,
  key numbers, compare options, timeline, decision request, flow diagram,
  quote, cause and effect, insight to action.
- Nothing is saved until they press Save, and nothing is published unless they
  explicitly review and confirm it (`/matsugit-publish`).

End by asking what they want to make. Do not open, edit or publish anything
just because help was requested.
