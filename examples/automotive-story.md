# What changes

## Sell the journey, not the spec sheet
### The next automotive advantage should make an ordinary Tuesday easier

| Industry promise | Driver question | Better experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Electric power | Can I get there? | A route with a credible charging plan |
| More software | Will this help me? | Useful updates, on my schedule |
| Driving assistance | What must I still do? | Clear limits and responsibilities |

> [!TAKEAWAY]
> Make the trip simpler, not the feature list longer.

::: source
Illustrative product thesis, not a company roadmap or customer-research result.
:::

## Three everyday jobs, not another feature list
### Every capability should earn its place in the driver's routine

:::: columns 1:1:1
#### Plan the trip
Bring charging into the route, with a useful alternative when plans change.

#### Respect the schedule
Explain the benefit of an update and let the driver choose a suitable time.

#### Make limits clear
Show what assistance can do, where it works and what remains the driver's job.
::::

> [!TAKEAWAY]
> Start with the journey, then choose the technology.

# What must stay simple

## Trust is a product feature
### A driver should never have to guess who is responsible

:::: columns 1:1
#### The design question
- Can the driver understand the current mode?
- Are limits and responsibilities visible?

#### The product response
- Explain what the system does and where it works.
- Test comprehension before expanding the promise.
::::

> [!TAKEAWAY]
> Never let the product name promise more than the system can do.

::: source
Illustrative design questions, not a safety validation or release process.
:::

# What to build next

## Less friction. More loyalty.
### Turn each technology promise into a practical driver benefit

::: {.mapping relation="suggests"}
| Driver friction | Product response |
| :--- | :--- |
| Charging takes planning. | Bring charging into route planning. |
| Updates interrupt routines. | Show the benefit and offer a suitable time. |
| Assistance feels ambiguous. | Explain availability, limits and driver duties. |
:::

> [!TAKEAWAY]
> Make capabilities earn their place in the daily routine.

## Ship a better Tuesday
### Prove one everyday improvement before expanding the promise

:::: timeline
#### Understand
Observe one journey, such as planning a charge stop.

#### Prototype
Test a clearer planning experience with drivers.

#### Decide
Check comprehension and task success before expanding.
::::

> [!TAKEAWAY]
> The car of the future should ask less of its driver.

::: source
Illustrative hypotheses to test, not measured customer outcomes.
:::
