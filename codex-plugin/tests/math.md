# Example

## Discount opportunity
### Three factors define the calculation

| Driver | Symbol | Value |
|---|:---:|---:|
| Exposure | $e$ | 20% |
| Discount | $d$ | 15% |
| Recovery | $g$ | 5% |

$$
\Delta R = R \times e \times \frac{d}{1-d} \times g
$$

$R$ is revenue; $\Delta R$ is recovered revenue.

## Recovery calculation
### An illustrative example

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Discounted sales} \quad &= 200 \times 20\% = 40 \\
\text{Ticket value} \quad &= \frac{40}{1-20\%} = 50 \\
\text{Gap} \quad &= 50 - 40 = 10 \\
\text{Recovery} \quad &= 10 \times 5\% = \boxed{0.5}
\end{aligned}
$$

The gap is $\frac{20\%}{80\%} = \frac{1}{4}$.
